## The short version

You answer a set of questions about how God has shaped you. Your answers stay
in your own browser until you decide to share them. If you share them, a small
number of ministry leaders can see them so they can talk to you about serving.
We keep them for a fixed period and then delete them automatically, and you can
ask us to delete them sooner at any time.

We do not use analytics, we do not set cookies of our own, and nothing you write
is sent anywhere except to Fellowship Dubai.

## Who is responsible

Fellowship Dubai. For anything in this notice, including asking for your
profile to be removed, write to **serve@fellowshipdubai.com**.

## What we collect, and when

### While you are answering — nothing reaches us

The journey saves your progress in your own browser, on your own device, using
its local storage. That is not a cookie and it is not sent to us. If you close
the tab and come back on the same device and browser, your answers are still
there. If you clear your browser data, they are gone and we never had them.

The one exception is described under *"Which step people stop at"* below.

### When you choose to share your profile

Only when you tick the consent box and press send does anything reach us. At
that point we receive and store:

- **Your name, email address and phone number.**
- **Roughly how long you expect to be in the UAE**, if you told us. This is
  optional, and exists so a leader does not suggest a role needing two years of
  continuity to someone here for six months.
- **Your full S.H.A.P.E. answers** — spiritual gifts, heart, abilities,
  personality and experiences, including anything you wrote in your own words.
  The Experiences section can include painful or difficult history if you chose
  to record it.
- **A record of the consent you gave**: the exact wording you agreed to, saved
  word for word, the version of it, and the retention period you were told. If
  we later change the wording, your record still shows what *you* agreed to,
  not what somebody agreed to afterwards.
- **A one-way scrambled form of your IP address and browser identifier**, stored
  with that consent record. These cannot be turned back into your address; they
  exist so we can show that a specific consent came from a specific session.

### When you ask us to save your place

If you ask to continue on another device, we store your unfinished answers, your
email address, and how far you had got, so we can send you a link back to them.
This is a separate request with its own wording — finishing the journey is not
the same as agreeing to share it, and an unfinished set of answers is not
something you have agreed to share with anyone.

A saved draft is kept for **30 days**, is deleted the moment you resume it, and
is deleted when you submit a finished profile. **No ministry leader can see
drafts.** They are not part of any list, search, export or email.

### Which step people stop at

The journey records a plain count of how many people reached each step — a
single number per step, nothing else. There is no name, no address, no session,
and no record of any individual's path through it. It answers "where do people
give up" and nothing else, so we can fix the step that is losing people.

To avoid counting the same person twice, that counter uses the scrambled form of
your IP address, held for a day and then discarded.

### Stopping abuse

The form accepts at most five submissions from one internet connection per hour,
using the same scrambled IP address. It also contains a field hidden from people
and visible to automated bots; if it is filled in, the submission is rejected.

## Who can see your profile

Two kinds of account, and they see different things.

- **Ministry leaders** see only profiles suggested to a team they lead. **The
  Experiences section is hidden from them entirely** — the page tells them it
  has been hidden rather than pretending it is not there.
- **Pastors** can see every confirmed profile including Experiences.

Until you open the confirmation link we email you, **your profile is invisible
to everyone**, including pastors and site administrators. It is not in any list,
not returned by any search, and cannot be reached by guessing.

**Every time somebody opens your profile it is recorded** — who, what and when —
and viewing the Experiences section is recorded separately. That record is kept
so that access to sensitive information can be accounted for.

If a leader writes notes of a conversation with you, those notes are stored
against your profile and deleted with it. The content of a note is never copied
into the access record.

---

## Emails

- **A confirmation email**, immediately, so we know the address is yours. Until
  you open it nothing is shared with anyone. The link works once and expires
  after 48 hours, and only a scrambled form of it is stored — a copy of our
  database would not let anyone confirm your address.
- **A link back to your answers**, only if you asked us to save your place.
- Ministry leaders receive **a weekly summary** of who is waiting to be
  contacted. It contains **names and dates only** — no gifts, no notes, no
  answers. Email is not a place to put a S.H.A.P.E. profile.

We do not send newsletters or marketing from this system.

Email is delivered by an external mail provider on Fellowship Dubai's behalf.
That provider handles the message in transit and its own privacy terms apply to
that step.

## How long we keep things

| | |
|---|---|
| A shared profile | **24 months**, then deleted automatically by a daily job |
| A profile whose email was never confirmed | 7 days |
| A saved draft | 30 days |
| The step counter | Indefinitely — it contains no personal data |
| The record of who viewed what | Kept after the profile is deleted |

Profiles older than 18 months are flagged to leaders as out of date, because a
profile that old no longer describes the person who filled it in. Flagged is not
deleted; deletion happens at 24 months.

> **For review:** 24 months is the current setting and can be changed. It is
> written into the consent wording each person agrees to, so changing it applies
> to people who consent afterwards, not to those who already have.

The record of who viewed a profile deliberately outlives the profile — including
the entry recording its deletion. It holds no names or answers, only which
account did what and when.

## Deleting your profile

Write to **serve@fellowshipdubai.com** and ask. A pastor can delete it, and doing
so removes the profile, the consent record, the team suggestions and every
conversation note, permanently and with no undo.

You do not have to give a reason, and it does not affect your welcome at
Fellowship Dubai in any way.

## Where your information goes

It is stored in Fellowship Dubai's own website database, on the church's
hosting. It is not sold, rented, or shared with any other organisation.

**Planning Center is not connected to this system.** No profile is copied to it,
nothing is read from it, and no credentials exist. The dashboard offers leaders
a link that opens a Planning Center search — following that link takes them
there, but nothing is sent.

### One exception, and it is worth knowing

At the end of the journey the page offers Fellowship Dubai's
serving-opportunities form, which is hosted by **Church Center (Planning
Center)**. You can open it in a new tab, or ask for it to be shown on the page.

Nothing is fetched from Planning Center until you do one of those things. If you
choose to show the form here, it is loaded from their servers, so at that point
Planning Center receives your IP address and may set its own cookies. Anything
you type into that form goes to Planning Center, not into this system, and
Planning Center's own privacy terms apply to it.

The form also goes somewhere different from the rest of the journey: if you fill
it in without sharing your profile, the SERVE team does not receive your
S.H.A.P.E. answers. The page says so where the form appears.

## What this system does not do

- **No cookies of our own.** The site sets none for this journey.
- **No analytics.** No Google Analytics, no tracking pixels, no session
  recording, no heatmaps, no advertising tags.
- **No external fonts, scripts or images.** The pages load nothing from any
  other company's servers, apart from the embedded form described above.
- **No photographs.**
- **No automated decisions.** The dashboard suggests teams and gives its reasons,
  but no software places anyone anywhere. A leader reads it, a conversation
  happens, and you decide.
- **No exporting of sensitive detail.** Leaders can export a spreadsheet of the
  profiles they can see. **Experiences and conversation notes are excluded from
  it at every level of access**, including a pastor's, because a spreadsheet is
  the easiest thing in the world to forward to the wrong person.
