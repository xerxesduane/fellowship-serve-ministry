# Using the SERVE dashboard

For ministry leaders at Fellowship Dubai. About fifteen minutes to read once,
and then you should not need it again.

Everything else in `docs/` is written for whoever installs and maintains the
site. This one is not.

---

## What this is for

Somebody in the congregation spends twenty minutes answering questions about how
God has shaped them — their gifts, what stirs them, what they can do, how they
are wired, what they have been through. At the end they can share that with the
SERVE team.

The dashboard is where you see the people who did, and where you keep track of
what happens next. That is all it is. It does not decide anything.

**Three things worth knowing before you start:**

- **A suggestion is a starting point, not an answer.** The dashboard compares
  someone's gifts and passions with what a team is like and offers a view. It is
  often useful and sometimes wrong. You are the one who has met people.
- **Nobody is placed by software.** The system suggests, you have the
  conversation, and the person decides. If that order ever feels reversed,
  something has gone wrong.
- **A team being short of people is never a reason to place someone.** The gap
  figures are context for a conversation, nothing more. The dashboard is built
  so that team need cannot influence who it suggests.

---

## Getting in

**SERVE** in the left-hand menu of the WordPress admin. If it is not there, ask
whoever set the site up to give your account a SERVE role.

There are two:

- **SERVE Ministry Leader** — you see people suggested to the team or teams you
  lead. You can move them along, claim follow-ups and write notes.
- **SERVE Pastor** — sees everyone, can record background checks, delete
  profiles and change settings.

If your dashboard is completely empty and you expected people in it, the usual
cause is that no team has been assigned to you yet. That is a one-line fix in
**Teams and gaps** — ask.

---

## The dashboard

Four figures across the top: profiles completed, people ready for a
conversation, follow-ups due, and people currently serving.

Beneath them:

- **People ready for a next step** — the main list. Click anybody to open them.
- **Serving team gaps** — where teams are short. Context only.
- **Quick follow-ups** — what is due today or overdue.
- **Settling in** — people you placed a while ago that nobody has been back to.
- **Something not working?** — see the last section. Please use it.

---

## Opening somebody

Clicking a name opens a panel on the right.

**Contact** — their email and phone, as links. Tap the number on a phone and it
dials.

**Journey status** — where they are, and whether a background check applies.

**Record what happened** — how you move them along. Pick a stage, and where the
stage is about a particular team, pick the team too:

| Stage | Means |
|---|---|
| Submitted | They have shared their profile; nobody has been in touch |
| Contacted | You have reached out |
| Conversation booked | A conversation is arranged |
| Trial serve | They are trying a team out |
| Placed | They are serving |
| Paused | Not now — needs a date to bring them back |
| Declined | Not this, or not at all |

Two of these ask for something extra. **Paused** needs a return date, because a
queue full of "travelling until September" with no dates is a queue nobody
opens. **Declined** offers a box for anything worth recording — it is optional
and it is not a judgement on them.

**S.H.A.P.E. profile** — what they said about themselves. If you are a ministry
leader you will see a note that the Experiences section is hidden from you. That
is deliberate; see below.

**Suggested teams** — with the reasons. Read the reasons rather than the
ordering. If the reasons do not convince you, they should not convince you.

**Who is following this up** — claim it so two of you do not ring the same
person on the same evening. Release it when you are done, or if you cannot get
to it.

**Conversation history** — write down what was said. This is the single most
useful thing you can do for whoever picks the person up after you. Notes are
added, never overwritten, so you are always adding to the story rather than
replacing it.

## Things that will stop you, and why

**"A cleared background check is required before moving anyone to Placed."**
Fellowship Kids and Youth Ministry work with under-18s. Only a pastor can record
a check. This is not negotiable and not a bug — ask a pastor.

**"Trial serve and Placed apply to a particular team."** Both are decisions about
a specific team, so the dashboard needs to know which one. Pick it from the list.

**Experiences are hidden from you.** That section can contain bereavement,
illness, addiction, abuse. A ministry leader does not need it to have a good
first conversation, and the fewer people who can read it the better. A pastor can
see it, and every time anybody does, it is recorded. If you genuinely need
something from it for a specific conversation, ask a pastor — do not work around
it.

## The rhythm

**Weekly.** You get one email on a Monday listing who is waiting. Names and
dates only — no gifts, no notes, no answers, because email is not a place to put
somebody's S.H.A.P.E. profile.

**When somebody new appears.** Claim them, ring them, write down what was said,
move the stage. The first conversation is scheduled three days out; that is a
nudge, not a deadline.

**Six weeks after you place somebody.** They appear under *Settling in*. Ask how
it is actually going. Somebody put on a team and never spoken to again is how a
willing volunteer quietly stops turning up. Press **Spoke to them** when you
have.

## Things people ask

**Someone says they never got the confirmation email.** Their profile is
invisible to everyone until they open it, and is deleted after seven days if
they do not. A pastor can resend from **Settings → Unsent confirmations**. If
this happens more than once, tell whoever runs the site — it usually means mail
is broken rather than that people are ignoring it.

**Someone wants their profile deleted.** They can write to
serve@fellowshipdubai.com, or you can ask a pastor, who can delete it from the
panel. It goes completely — profile, consent record, team suggestions and notes.
There is no undo, they do not have to give a reason, and it does not affect
their welcome in any way.

**Someone's profile is old.** Profiles over eighteen months are flagged as stale,
because a profile that old no longer describes the person who filled it in. Treat
it as a conversation starter, not a record. Everything is deleted automatically
at twenty-four months.

**Somebody I know is not in the list.** Either they have not shared their profile
(finishing the journey and sharing it are two separate steps), or they are not
suggested to a team you lead, or they have not confirmed their email. A pastor
can see everyone.

## When it is wrong

It will be. The suggestions are built on comparing words, not on knowing anybody.

Use **Something not working?** on the dashboard. It takes a category and a
sentence, and goes to whoever is running the pilot. It is about the tool rather
than about a person, nobody is judged by it, and — this is the important part —
the numbers cannot tell anyone that a suggestion made no sense. Only you can.

If a suggestion is wrong, saying so is more useful than working around it.
